# Shipping & Delivery FAQ
**Last Updated:** June 10, 2024 | **Owner:** Fulfillment Team
**Audience:** Customers

## Delivery Options
| Tier | Delivery Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $5.99 (free on orders $50+) |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $14.99 |

Delivery times begin when the order **ships**, not when it is placed. Orders typically ship within 24 hours (1 business day).

## Free Shipping
Orders of **$50 or more** (before tax) qualify for free Standard shipping. Express is never free.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once your order ships. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show updates.

## Delivery Area
We deliver to all 50 U.S. states and U.S. territories.

## Lost Package Claims
- If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not receive it, wait 48 hours, then check with neighbors and your household.
- If a package has had **no tracking movement for 7 business days**, or is not received **10 business days after the ship date**, you may file a lost package claim.
- Approved claims receive a free replacement or a full refund, at the customer's choice.
- Claims must be filed within **30 days of the ship date**.
