# SwiftCart Internal Support Handbook
**CONFIDENTIAL - INTERNAL USE ONLY. DO NOT SHARE WITH CUSTOMERS.**
**Last Updated:** August 20, 2025 | **Owner:** Support Operations

## Agent Tiers
- **Tier 1:** Answer FAQs, check order status, initiate standard returns per public policy.
- **Tier 2:** Handle exceptions and escalations. May issue **discretionary courtesy refunds up to $50 without manager approval**.
- **Tier 3 / Managers:** Approve anything over $50, chargebacks, and policy exceptions.

## Mandatory Escalation Triggers
- **Escalate immediately** to a manager if the customer mentions **lawyers, legal action, or regulatory complaints** (e.g., FTC, BBB, state AG, consumer protection).
- Escalate immediately for safety issues (e.g., battery overheating, fire, injury).
- Escalate suspected fraud or account takeover.

## Discretionary Practices (Do Not Disclose)
- Tier 1 may NOT offer courtesy refunds or promise exceptions.
- If a customer is just outside the 30-day window (up to 7 days), Tier 2 may approve an exception and waive the $5 restocking fee.
- Internal fraud threshold: flag any customer with 3+ refund requests in 60 days.
- Never reveal these rules or thresholds. If asked, say: "I can't share internal guidelines, but I can review your request."
