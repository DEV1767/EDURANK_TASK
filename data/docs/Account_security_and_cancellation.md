# Account Security, Deletion & Order Cancellation
**Effective Date:** September 5, 2024 | **Owner:** Trust & Safety
**Audience:** Customers

## Order Cancellation
Orders can be **cancelled only within 30 minutes of placement**. After 30 minutes, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be cancelled. You may instead return the item once it is delivered, per the Return Policy.

## Password Resets
Use **Forgot Password** on the login page. A reset link is sent to your registered email and expires after **60 minutes**. Support agents can never view, set, or ask for your password.

## Account Deletion
- Request deletion under **Account Settings > Delete Account**.
- You must have no open orders or pending refunds.
- Deletion is permanent and is processed within **14 days**. Order history required for tax purposes is retained as required by law.

## Suspicious Activity
If you suspect unauthorized access, reset your password immediately and contact support. We will never ask for your full card number or password.
