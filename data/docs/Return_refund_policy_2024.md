# SwiftCart Return & Refund Policy
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024 | **Last Reviewed:** March 1, 2024 | **Owner:** Customer Experience Team
**Audience:** Customers (public)

## 1. Return Window
You may return most items within **30 days of the delivery date**.

## 2. Eligibility
- Items must be **unopened**, unused, and in original packaging.
- Proof of purchase (order ID) is required.
- Final-sale items (marked "Final Sale" on the product page) cannot be returned.

## 3. Restocking Fee
- Electronics are subject to a **$5.00 restocking fee** per item, deducted from the refund.
- All other categories have no restocking fee.

## 4. Return Shipping
- Customers pay for return shipping unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.
- A prepaid label is provided for defective-item returns only.

## 5. Refund Processing
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** after the returned item is received and inspected.
- Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

## 6. How to Start a Return
Visit **My Orders > Request Return**, or contact support with your order ID.
