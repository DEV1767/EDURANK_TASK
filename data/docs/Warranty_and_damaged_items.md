# Warranty & Damaged Items Policy
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024 | **Last Updated:** September 9, 2024 | **Owner:** Quality Assurance
**Audience:** Customers

## 1-Year Manufacturer Warranty
Products carry a **1-year manufacturer warranty** against **defective goods** (manufacturing faults). The warranty begins on the delivery date. It does not cover misuse, accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.

## Damaged on Arrival
If an item arrives broken or damaged in transit, you must report it **within 48 hours of delivery** and include **clear photos** of the item and the packaging. Claims outside this window may be declined.

## Resolution
For approved claims, we will offer a replacement or a full refund (including shipping).

## Warranty Claims (after 48 hours)
For defects discovered later (within the 1-year warranty period), contact support with your order ID, a description of the fault, and photos or video. We will coordinate with the manufacturer. Shipping on approved warranty returns is prepaid.
