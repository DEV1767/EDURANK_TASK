# SwiftCart Returns Policy (v1.2)
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2022 | **Last Updated:** November 3, 2022 | **Owner:** Operations
**Audience:** Customers

Welcome to SwiftCart! We want you to love your purchase.

## Return Window
You have **60 days** from the date of delivery to return any item, no questions asked.

## Condition of Items
Items may be returned opened or unopened as long as they are in reasonable condition.

## Fees
**All returns are free.** There are no restocking fees on any product category, including electronics. We also provide a free prepaid return label on every return.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 10 business days. Original shipping fees are refunded in full.
