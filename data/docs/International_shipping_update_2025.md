# MEMO: International Shipping Expansion
**Date:** February 3, 2025 | **From:** Head of Logistics | **Status:** ACTIVE
**Audience:** Customers & Support Staff

Effective **February 17, 2025**, SwiftCart ships to **Canada** and the **United Kingdom**.

## Details
- Standard international shipping: **7-14 business days**. Express international is not yet available.
- Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.
- The $50 free shipping threshold applies to **U.S. orders only**.
- **Customs duties, import taxes, and brokerage fees are the responsibility of the customer** and are collected by the carrier at delivery or paid by the customer.
- Refused or unclaimed shipments due to unpaid duties are not eligible for a refund of shipping or duties.

## Not Yet Supported
All other countries remain unsupported at this time.
